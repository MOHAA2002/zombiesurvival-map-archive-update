Alright, i've pimped out Oviscity. None of that faggoty radish picking junk and custom textures anymore.
I've made most houses accessible, but i've made their interiors more limited. There is a lot of ammo on the map, but you have to move around if you wanna find it.
I'd say this map is almost as easy as hamlet, except there's none of those exploitable ammo boxes that give you eleventy-bake gallons of ammo.
There is only one health charger - that's right - at the hospital, which i limited to one floor. There are a few healthvials and packs strewn across the city, but they won't work well unless you move around the city a lot. The player zombies spawn all over the city in alleys and dark corners. The PD is accessible, but the prison cells are permanently closed.
There are like 10 frags on the map, but they're hidden pretty well. Most of them, anyways.
Hope the map works better than rambocity.

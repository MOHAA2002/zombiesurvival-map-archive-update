Description
------------
This version of deadblock is an update to deadblock_v1, which was small in comparison. You are in a town, medium in size, with zombies all around you. The player zombie spawns are protected by a wall players can't get above (no more spawncamping). The map has 1 health machine, with a few dispersed health kits. I added realistic lighting effects (unlike deadblock_v1), and different colored lights. There is 6 possible cade rooms, each with props inside to help you. I dispersed 3 or 4 hammers across the map. Last but not least, I added player clip around certain parts of the map to prevent exploit issues. This is my 7th map I've made for zs, and hopefully my best so far.

*This map was intended for DeathLegends ZS server*

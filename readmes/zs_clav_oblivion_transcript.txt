My first real map made with Hammer, meant to be used in the Zombie Survival gamemode by JetBoom. Features a not-too-large open area where players have limited visibility due to fog and large pieces of scrap metal (mainly trains :). A few 'buildings' (more bunkers) are in the area, with multiple entrances to prevent players from keeping their guns fixed at one entrance.

Map created by ClavusElite. See you on the Mr-Green server! (unquestionable one of the most popular ZS servers).

This is an edit of abandoned mall version whatever, modified so that every store is a starbucks. This project started in 2018 and was hosted as a joke map on a handful of zs servers. It's been updated a few times, but never officially released until now.

The joke is that the zombies get faster every wave. If your zs server has any sense of balance— it should play fine.

I challenge you to find a more optimized abandoned mall.

Features

    38 possible sigil locations
    Large, semi-open mall to explore
    Unfathomable amount of props available for barricading
    A lot of caffeine
